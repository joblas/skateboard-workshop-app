Tip of the Day · 4h ago
Heelflip mechanics 👊
When learning heelflips, focus on the heel-side edge of your front foot. The flick angle is everything — your foot should exit at about 45° off the board nose. Most people flick too late. Pop first, THEN flick. Video breakdown dropping this week.
